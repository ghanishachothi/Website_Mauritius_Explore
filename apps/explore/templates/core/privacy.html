Mauritius Explore is a travel discovery website for the island of Mauritius, built as a university project at the University of Mauritius. This page explains what information we collect when you use the site, why we collect it, and what control you have over it.

01 Information we collect

We only collect what the site needs in order to work:

  • Account details - your username, email address and password when you create an account. Passwords are stored encrypted and nobody, including our team, can read them.
  • Messages you send us - the name, email address and message you type into the Help & Contact form.
  • Bookings and saved items - the experiences you reserve, review or add to your favourites.
  • Basic technical information - the pages you open, so the site can show you the right content.

We do not ask for identity card numbers, bank card numbers or any payment details on this site.

02 Why we use it

  • To let you log in and see your own bookings and saved experiences.
  • To reply to the questions you send through the contact form.
  • To show you the right information, such as the experiences you have reserved.
  • To keep the site working correctly and to fix problems.

We never sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

03 Cookies and sessions

We use a small session cookie so the site remembers that you are logged in while you move between pages, and a security cookie that protects forms from being submitted by other websites. These are necessary for the site to work. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your browser may also store small preferences on your device, such as a filter you selected. You can clear these at any time from your browser settings.

04 Who can see it

Only the Mauritius Explore project team can see the information stored on the site, and only through the administration area, which requires a staff account.

Some pages show a map of Mauritius provided by Google Maps. When that map loads, Google may receive technical information about your visit, under its own privacy policy.

We share nothing else with anyone, and we never transfer your details to another company.

05 How long we keep it

  • Account details - until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Contact messages - until the question has been answered and the project is assessed.
  • Bookings and reviews - while your account exists, so you can see your own history.

06 Keeping it safe

Passwords are stored using a one-way encryption method, so they cannot be read back. Administration pages are protected by login, and only team members with a staff account can reach them. As this is a student project running on university infrastructure, please do not enter sensitive personal information you would not want stored.

07 Your rights

Under the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your account and the information attached to it;
  • stop using your information for a particular purpose.

Write to us through the Help & Contact form and we will answer within a few days.

08 Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. For any question about your information, use the Help & Contact form, or write to us at Rose Hill, Mauritius.
